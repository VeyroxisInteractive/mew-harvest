# MEW HARVEST — Island Life

50 offline additions and extensions, organised into nine menu sections.
Existing fishing, bee gardens, room furnishing, farm layouts and expeditions remain in their original menus.

1. **Crop crossing** (garden) — Cross wheat and corn into hybrid seed for a higher-yield nursery bed.

2. **Seed bank** (garden) — Deposit and withdraw crop seed packets without losing stock.

3. **Soil testing** (garden) — Inspect the previous crop and the current rotation/mulch benefits of every field.

4. **Crop rotation** (garden) — Alternate crop families for a ten-percent growth-time reduction.

5. **Field mulch** (garden) — Apply compost to an empty field for one extra item at its next harvest.

6. **Beeswax harvest** (garden) — Gather wax from your existing bee garden after a timed flower-fed cycle.

7. **Candle making** (garden) — Turn beeswax and fiber into saleable candles.

8. **Bee pollination** (garden) — Use flowers to pollinate a growing field for one bonus crop.

9. **Cutting nursery** (garden) — Grow an apple sapling from fruit and compost for the existing tree system.

10. **Fruit grafting** (garden) — Graft a fruit tree for one bonus fruit at its next harvest.

11. **Tidal shoreline** (shore) — An island-clock tide changes every five minutes and reveals gathering windows.

12. **Beachcombing** (shore) — Search the low-tide beach for shells and an occasional pearl.

13. **Beach cleanup** (shore) — Collect litter and improve a persistent beach cleanliness score.

14. **Material recycling** (shore) — Sort recovered litter into usable fiber and compost.

15. **Rock-pool rescue** (shore) — At low tide, return stranded crabs to the water and earn conservation tokens.

16. **Cheese cellar** (cellar) — Age existing goat cheese into a higher-value artisan cheese.

17. **Pickling jars** (cellar) — Preserve carrots and herbs into jars of pickles.

18. **Jam preserving** (cellar) — Cook apples and honey into shelf-stable jam.

19. **Maturation choice** (cellar) — Collect cheese young or leave it longer for a reserve batch.

20. **Tasting board** (cellar) — Fulfil rotating preserved-food tasting orders for coins.

21. **Guest bookings** (guests) — Welcome a named visitor to an existing guest cottage.

22. **Guest comfort** (guests) — Install bedding to upgrade the quality of future visits.

23. **Guest meals** (guests) — Choose and serve a meal; matching visitor tastes raises satisfaction.

24. **Postcard collection** (guests) — Collect visitor postcards after successful stays.

25. **Guest preferences** (guests) — Choose activities suited to each visitor for a larger checkout reward.

26. **Mine grid** (mine) — Choose adjacent chambers on a persistent four-by-four mine map.

27. **Lantern fuel** (mine) — Craft and spend oil to explore deeper chambers.

28. **Support braces** (mine) — Build supports to enter unstable chambers safely.

29. **Ore refining** (mine) — Refine mine ore into iron for existing construction recipes.

30. **Mineral exhibits** (mine) — Donate distinct minerals to unlock a one-time museum reward.

31. **Wildlife admissions** (sanctuary) — Admit a hedgehog, rabbit or owl into your wildlife sanctuary.

32. **Rescue feeding** (sanctuary) — Provide food suited to the current rescued animal.

33. **Wildlife treatment** (sanctuary) — Use herbs and time to nurse an animal back to health.

34. **Wildlife release** (sanctuary) — Release a fed, recovered animal for conservation tokens.

35. **Habitat upgrades** (sanctuary) — Spend conservation tokens on safer habitats that shorten recovery.

36. **Butterfly lures** (nature) — Choose a flower or fruit lure that attracts different species.

37. **Quiet observation** (nature) — Watch a timed visit without disturbing it to record a species.

38. **Butterfly collection** (nature) — Complete a recorded species set for a one-time reward.

39. **Firefly watch** (nature) — Record fireflies during the island-clock night window.

40. **Nature commissions** (nature) — Turn conservation tokens into rewards through repeatable ecology requests.

41. **Map assembly** (treasure) — Combine beach map scraps into a treasure map.

42. **Clue solving** (treasure) — Read a directional clue and choose its matching landmark.

43. **Treasure digging** (treasure) — Dig at the solved landmark for coins and a relic.

44. **Relic restoration** (treasure) — Clean and restore recovered fragments with a timed workshop action.

45. **Relic gallery** (treasure) — Exhibit three restored relics for a permanent completion reward.

46. **Music sequencer** (play) — Compose, save and play an eight-note tune locally.

47. **Cooking rhythm** (play) — Follow a three-step cooking sequence to earn a skill-based bonus.

48. **Farm contests** (play) — Submit a crop sample and compete against local NPC scores.

49. **Valley hot springs** (play) — Send an idle cat to unlocked Sunrise Valley for a friendship boost.

50. **Village council choices** (play) — Choose a permanent farming, trade or conservation policy with a real effect.

Access: Menu → Island Life. Most activities unlock at levels 2–10; hot springs require the unlocked level-50 valley. These activities use menu scenes and existing farm inventory. Online friend visits and co-op are not part of this release.
